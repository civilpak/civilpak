Budget Planning

1 Kanal Ghar Ka Budget Kaise Banayen

7 min read · Civil Engineering Notes

Budget banate waqt sab se badi ghalti ye hoti hai ke sirf material aur labor ka hisaab lagaya jata hai — baaki cheezein bhool jati hain jo baad mein cost overrun ka sabab banti hain. Neeche ek practical framework hai.

1. Sub-Structure (Foundation)

Total budget ka taqreeban 12-15% foundation par jata hai — is mein excavation, steel, cement aur soil condition k hisaab se depth shamil hai.

2. Super-Structure (Grey Structure)

Ye sab se bara hissa hai — 40-45% budget. Isme columns, beams, slabs, aur brickwork shamil hai. Yahan quality compromise sab se zyada nuksaan deta hai, is liye is portion mein bachat na karein.

3. Finishing (Flooring, Paint, Fixtures)

Finishing budget ka 25-30% le leta hai. Ye wo jaga hai jahan aap apni choice k hisaab se adjust kar sakte hain — tiles, paint grade aur fixtures ki range wide hoti hai.

4. Electrical aur Plumbing

Roughly 10-12% budget. Quality wiring aur pipes par compromise na karein — ye baad mein tor-phor kar k replace karna bohat mehnga hota hai.

Engineer's Note Har budget mein 10-15% **contingency** zaroor rakhein — material price fluctuation, design changes ya unexpected site conditions is se cover ho jate hain.

5. Labor Costs

Labor rate area aur skill level k hisaab se vary karta hai. Contractor se hamesha written quotation lein jisme scope of work clearly likha ho.

Budget Tracking Tools Construction budget tracking spreadsheets aur measuring tools yahan Daraz par dekhein.

Sample Breakdown (1 Kanal)

  • Foundation: 12-15%
  • Grey Structure: 40-45%
  • Finishing: 25-30%
  • Electrical/Plumbing: 10-12%
  • Contingency: 10-15% (extra)

Aakhri Baat

Budget banate waqt hamesha ek qualified engineer ya quantity surveyor se BOQ (Bill of Quantities) taiyaar karwayein — ye aapko exact material quantities aur cost ka clear andaza deta hai, guesswork nahi.

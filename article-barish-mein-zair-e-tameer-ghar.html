Barish K Mausam Mein Zair-e-Tameer Ghar Ki Ehtiyat

6 min read · Civil Engineering Notes

Agar aapka ghar abhi construction phase mein hai aur barish ka mausam shuru ho gaya hai, to ye waqt sab se zyada nazuk hota hai. Kaam rukwana behtar hai bajaye is k ke jaldi mein aisi ghalti ho jaye jo baad mein permanent nuksaan bana de. Neeche wo ehtiyat hain jo har stage par zaroori hain.

1. Cement aur Bags Ko Namee Se Bachayein

Cement bags zameen se upar, platform par aur plastic sheet se dhak kar rakhein. Geeli cement apni strength kho deti hai aur use karne k qabil nahi rehti — bhale wo bag khula na bhi ho, humidity apna asar dikha deti hai.

2. Khuli Foundation Ko Cover Karein

Agar foundation abhi khuli hai (backfill nahi hui), to us mein paani jama hone se soil ka bearing capacity kam ho sakta hai. Tarpaulin ya plastic sheet se cover karein aur agar paani jama ho jaye to kaam shuru karne se pehle usay nikaal dein.

Engineer's Note Freshly cast concrete (columns, beams, slab) ko barish ka seedha paani lagne se bachayein — ye curing process ko disturb kar sakta hai aur surface weak reh sakti hai.

3. Steel Bars Ko Rust Se Bachayein

Site par pari steel bars agar barish mein bheegti rahen to jaldi rust lag jata hai. Elevated platform par rakhein aur cover kar dein — halka surface rust to acceptable hai, lekin lagatar bheegna structural strength kam kar sakta hai.

4. Site Drainage Ka Intezam Karein

Site k around temporary drainage channel bana lein taake barish ka paani foundation ki taraf na aaye. Khaas taur par agar plot thora neechay hai to ye step zaroori hai warna paani ander hi khara reh jayega.

Engineer's Note Agar concrete casting ka plan hai aur barish ka forecast ho, to kaam postpone kar dein. Fresh concrete par barish girne se surface mein cracks aur weak spots ban sakte hain.

5. Bricks Aur Masonry Material Ko Cover Karein

Bricks agar lagatar barish mein bheegti rahen to unki strength aur shape par asar parta hai. Stack ki hui bricks ko plastic sheet se dhak kar rakhein, khaas kar raat ko jab site par koi nahi hota.

6. Electrical Aur Scaffolding Ka Khayal Rakhein

Temporary electrical connections aur scaffolding barish mein risky ho jate hain. Barish k dauran koi bhi electrical kaam band kar dein, aur scaffolding ki grip check kar lein k geeli lakri ya pipe phisalne ka khatra to nahi.

Site Protection Essentials Tarpaulin sheets, waterproof covers aur drainage tools yahan Daraz par dekhein.

Aakhri Baat

Barish k mausam mein construction rokna kabhi kabhi sab se sahi faisla hota hai. Jaldi mein kiya gaya kaam agar barish se kharab ho jaye to us ki repair costing naye kaam se bhi zyada ho sakti hai. Site engineer ya contractor se mausam dekh kar hi agla step plan karein.

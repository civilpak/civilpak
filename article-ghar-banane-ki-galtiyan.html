Ghar Banate Waqt 10 Aam Galtiyan Jo Log Karte Hain

6 min read · Civil Engineering Notes

Pakistan mein ghar banana zindagi ka sab se bara investment hota hai — aur yehi wo jaga hai jahan chhoti chhoti ghaltiyan lakhon rupay ka nuksaan bana deti hain. Site pe kaam karte hue maine ye galtiyan baar baar dohrati dekhi hain. Neeche unmein se 10 sab se aam aur sab se mehngi galtiyan hain.

1. Soil Testing Skip Karna

Foundation dalne se pehle soil test na karwana sab se common galti hai. Har plot ki mitti alag hoti hai — bina test k foundation design karna crack aur settlement ka risk barha deta hai.

2. Sasta Cement Use Karna

Cement pe bachat karna sab se mehnga faisla sabit hota hai. Structure ki strength directly cement quality se judi hai — is par compromise na karein.

Engineer's Note Har structural material (cement, steel, sand) ka ek certified brand aur consistent supplier rakhein — batch se batch quality change hone se structure weak ho sakta hai.

3. Proper Drawing K Bagair Construction Shuru Karna

Contractor ki verbal guidance pe ghar banana risky hai. Approved architectural aur structural drawings hone se material waste kam hota hai aur future mein renovation aasan rehta hai.

4. Waterproofing Ko Nazarandaz Karna

Roof aur bathroom waterproofing pe bachat karna baad mein seepage aur repair ka permanent kharcha bana deta hai. Ye ek-time investment hai jo dahaiyon tak fayda deta hai.

5. Electrical aur Plumbing Layout Pehle Se Plan Na Karna

Wall banane k baad pipe ya wiring k liye tor-phor karna time aur paisa dono zaya karta hai. Layout finalize karke hi masonry shuru karein.

Tools Jo Kaam Aayenge Waterproofing aur finishing k liye quality tools yahan Daraz par dekhein — verified sellers se order karein.

6–10: Baaki Galtiyan (Summary)

  • Ventilation ko design mein priority na dena
  • Contractor se written agreement na karna
  • Material delivery ki quality check na karna
  • Budget mein contingency (10-15%) na rakhna
  • Municipal approval se pehle construction shuru karna

Aakhri Baat

In galtiyon se bachne ka sabse aasan tareeqa ye hai ke construction shuru karne se pehle ek qualified civil engineer se drawings aur budget review karwa lein. Chhota sa upfront kharcha, baad ki bohat badi sar dard se bacha sakta hai.

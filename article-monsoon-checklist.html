Monsoon Se Pehle Ghar Ki 8 Checks

5 min read · Civil Engineering Notes

Barsaat shuru hone k baad problem pakarna mushkil aur mehnga hota hai. Behtar hai ke monsoon se pehle ye 8 checks complete kar lein — chhota sa maintenance kaam, bara nuksaan bacha sakta hai.

1. Roof Waterproofing Check Karein

Roof par koi crack ya damaged waterproofing layer hai to abhi repair karwayein — barish mein ye seepage ka sabab banti hai.

2. Drainage Pipes Saaf Karein

Roof aur terrace k drainage pipes mein pattay ya malba jama ho to pehle nikal dein, warna paani rukega aur andar seepage karega.

3. Wall Cracks Seal Karein

Bahar ki deewaron par koi hairline crack ho to usay seal kar dein — barish ka paani in cracks se andar ja kar bara damage kar sakta hai.

4. Windows aur Doors Ki Sealing Check Karein

Rubber seals ya weatherstripping purani ho gayi ho to replace karein taake barish ka paani andar na aaye.

Engineer's Note Terrace aur balcony ka slope check karein — paani ko drain ki taraf flow karna chahiye, agar kahin paani khara reh raha hai to ye future leakage ka sign hai.

5. Electrical Points Ko Moisture Se Bachayein

Exposed wiring ya switchboards jo bahar ya khule area mein hain, unhe waterproof covers se protect karein.

6. Basement/Ground Floor Waterproofing

Agar basement hai to uski waterproofing zaroor check karayein — ye sab se zyada affected area hoti hai heavy rain mein.

Monsoon Prep Essentials Waterproofing sealants, drainage tools aur silicone sealant yahan Daraz par dekhein.

7. Gutter aur Downspouts Inspect Karein

Blocked gutters overflow ho kar walls par paani gira dete hain jo paint aur plaster kharab karta hai.

8. Generator/Backup Electrical Setup Test Karein

Monsoon mein load-shedding barh jati hai — backup setup pehle se test kar lena behtar hai.

Aakhri Baat

Ye checklist saal mein ek baar, monsoon shuru hone se 3-4 hafte pehle complete kar lein. Chhota sa proactive maintenance, barsaat k mausam mein bare stress aur kharche se bacha deta hai.
